# Foreign Words & Phrases worksheet — answer key

## Part A. Fill in the blank

| # | Sentence | Options | Answer | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The politician's speech was dismissed as mere ________, lacking any concrete solutions. | Ad nauseam / Ad hoc / Alma mater / Vox populi | **Ad hoc** | Ambiguous item — none fits perfectly. 'Ad hoc' (improvised, no long-term plan) is the best of the four. |
| 2 | The treaty was signed ________, ensuring privacy away from the media's eyes. | In camera / In situ / In extremis / In loco parentis | **In camera** | In camera = in private. |
| 3 | The lawyer filed a petition of ________ to secure the prisoner's release. | Habeas corpus / Mea culpa / Corpus delicti / Ipso facto | **Habeas corpus** | Writ to bring a detained person before court. |
| 4 | His sudden arrival acted as a kind of ________, resolving the narrative too neatly. | Deus ex machina / Magnum opus / Ad libitum / Caveat emptor | **Deus ex machina** | An unexpected event that neatly resolves a plot. |
| 5 | Though she lacked experience, her boss gave her ________ to handle the project independently. | Carte blanche / Tabula rasa / Pax Romana / Prima facie | **Carte blanche** | Full freedom to act. |
| 6 | Philosophers often debate whether morality is relative or whether it exists ________. | A priori / Ad hoc / Sub judice / In absentia | **A priori** | Known through reason, independent of experience. |
| 7 | His biography is widely regarded as his ________, the most important work of his career. | Magnum opus / Tour de force / Corpus delicti / Status quo | **Magnum opus** | One's greatest work. |
| 8 | Their romance embodied the spirit of the ________, full of indulgence and pleasure. | Dolce vita / In vino veritas / Vox populi / Panacea | **Dolce vita** | The sweet life of pleasure. |
| 9 | He was known as a ________, constantly prying into others' business. | Bête noire / Alter ego / Non sequitur / Noli me tangere | **Bête noire** | None fits 'prying busybody' (that is 'quidnunc'). Bête noire is the expected pick. |
| 10 | The court proceedings emphasized the voice of the people, or the ________. | Vox populi / In medias res / Au revoir / Mea culpa | **Vox populi** | Voice of the people. |
| 11 | The rebellion spread rapidly, creating a sense of ________, or lawless confusion. | En masse / Coup d'état / Ad hoc / Ennui | **Coup d'état** | Closest to a rebellion; 'lawless confusion' itself would be anarchy. |
| 12 | Despite criticism, he continued to work, convinced that history would prove him ________. | De jure / In toto / Bona fide / Sui generis | **Bona fide** | Genuine — history would prove him genuine. |
| 13 | She justified her decision by saying it was made ________, in the heat of the moment. | Ad hoc / Ad nauseam / Post mortem / Raison d'être | **Ad hoc** | Made for this moment only, on the spot. |
| 14 | The teacher reminded students not to plagiarise, since academic honesty is a matter of ________. | Bona fide / Ipso facto / Mea culpa / Ad libitum | **Bona fide** | Good faith. |
| 15 | His alma mater invited him to deliver a convocation speech after two decades. | Alma mater / Alter ego / Corpus delicti / Vox populi | **Alma mater** | Already filled in on the sheet — one's old school/university. |
| 16 | The king ruled with ________, making decisions based only on personal will. | Laissez-faire / De facto / Tabula rasa / Persona non grata | **De facto** | Power in practice, not by law. (Laissez-faire = hands-off, the opposite.) |
| 17 | The scientist's discovery was published in a journal known for its motto “______” meaning “Truth conquers all.” | Veritas vincit / Sic transit gloria mundi / In vino veritas / Carpe diem | **Veritas vincit** | Truth conquers. |
| 18 | During the Renaissance, many thinkers believed in the value of “______,” or the pursuit of knowledge for its own sake. | Sine qua non / Summum bonum / Panacea / Pax Romana | **Summum bonum** | The highest good. |
| 19 | He was praised for being a true ________, a citizen of the world. | Cosmopolitan / Tabula rasa / Mea culpa / Persona non grata | **Cosmopolitan** | Citizen of the world. |
| 20 | The witness testified, but it was clear that he spoke only ________, not the entire truth. | In toto / Verbatim / Sub judice / Ipso facto | **In toto** | In toto = entirely; the sentence says he did NOT speak the entire truth. |
| 21 | The law stands firm, regardless of individual opinions, under the doctrine of ________. | De jure / Ad hoc / Prima facie / En route | **De jure** | By law. |
| 22 | The general was admired for his ________, or presence of mind during a crisis. | Savoir-faire / Mea culpa / Erratum / Ad infinitum | **Savoir-faire** | Presence of mind, knowing how to act. |
| 23 | He accused the journalist of writing ________, a personal attack rather than objective reporting. | Obiter dictum / Bête noire / Ad hominem / Status quo | **Ad hominem** | Attacking the person, not the argument. |
| 24 | The debate quickly descended into ________, with both sides refusing to listen. | Non sequitur / Coup d'état / Mea culpa / Ennui | **Non sequitur** | Statements that don't follow — a pointless back-and-forth. |
| 25 | Her theory remained merely ________, until supported by empirical evidence. | A priori / Corpus delicti / Sic transit gloria mundi / Vox populi | **A priori** | Theoretical, not yet tested by experience. |
| 26 | The scholar emphasized the principle of “______,” or the greatest good for the greatest number. | Summum bonum / Pax Romana / In extremis / Tabula rasa | **Summum bonum** | Highest/greatest good. |
| 27 | The monk lived by the motto “______,” which means “remember you will die.” | Memento mori / Mea culpa / Requiem / Ultimatum | **Memento mori** | Remember you will die. |
| 28 | The court declared the contract void ________, because it violated fundamental rights. | Ex post facto / Ipso facto / Ad libitum / In absentia | **Ipso facto** | By that very fact. |
| 29 | The CEO enjoyed complete authority and often acted as a true ________. | Autocrat / Alter ego / Persona non grata / Vox populi | **Autocrat** | Ruler with absolute power. |
| 30 | The defence argued that the confession was obtained ________, under duress. | In extremis / Pro tempore / Ex gratia / In situ | **In extremis** | Under extreme pressure/duress. |

Items 1, 9, 11 and 16 are loosely worded on the sheet — the note gives the best pick and why.

## Part B. Use in a sentence (model answers)

| Term | Meaning | Sentence |
|---|---|---|
| **Quasi** | As if; seemingly, partly | The students' union is a quasi-official body — recognised, but with no legal powers. |
| **Panacea** | A cure-all | Technology is not a panacea for every problem in education. |
| **Caveat** | A warning or condition | The scholarship is generous, with one caveat: you must keep a 7.5 CGPA. |
| **Hubris** | Excessive pride | The champion's hubris made him skip practice, and he lost in the first round. |
| **Nota bene** | Note well (N.B.) | Nota bene: late submissions will not be accepted. |
| **Hyperbole** | Deliberate exaggeration | Saying 'I've told you a million times' is hyperbole. |
| **En masse** | All together; as a group | The workers resigned en masse after the pay cut. |
| **Et al.** | And others | The study by Sharma et al. was cited in the report. |
| **Faux pas** | A social blunder | Wearing shoes inside the temple was an embarrassing faux pas. |
| **Habeas data** | Right to see and correct personal data held about you | Citing habeas data, she asked the bank to show all the personal information it held on her. |
| **Quorum** | Minimum number of members needed to make decisions | The meeting was postponed because there was no quorum. |
| **Doppelgänger** | A double; a look-alike | I met my doppelgänger at the airport — we even wore the same jacket. |
| **De facto** | In practice (though not by law) | Although never appointed, she is the de facto leader of the team. |
| **Per se** | By itself; intrinsically | Money per se does not bring happiness. |
| **Guerrilla** | Small independent fighter / irregular warfare | The guerrilla fighters hid in the forest and attacked at night. |
| **Prima facie** | At first sight; on the face of it | Prima facie, the evidence suggests the accused was at the scene. |
| **Quorum** | (repeated on the sheet) | A quorum of five members is needed to pass any resolution. |
| **Fiat** | An authoritative decree or order | The new rule was imposed by government fiat, without any debate. |
| **Curriculum vitae** | Course of life; a résumé (CV) | Please attach your curriculum vitae to the job application. |
| **Carpe diem** | Seize the day | Carpe diem — apply for the internship today instead of waiting. |
| **Consensus** | General agreement | The committee reached a consensus after two hours of discussion. |
| **Id est (i.e.)** | That is | Submit the form by the deadline, i.e., before Friday 5 p.m. |
| **Status quo** | The existing state of affairs | The workers want change, but the management prefers the status quo. |
| **Erratum** | An error (in print) | The publisher issued an erratum correcting the date on page 12. |
| **Veni, vidi, vici** | I came, I saw, I conquered | She walked into the chess tournament and won every game — veni, vidi, vici. |
